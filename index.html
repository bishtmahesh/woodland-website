Meet Your Principal Dentist

Dr. Sowjanya Avireddi

Principal Dentist · LDS RCS Eng, MFDS RCS Edin

Dr. Sowjanya completed her BDS in 2008 before gaining over 7 years of hospital experience in Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery and Oral Medicine at leading NHS Foundation Trusts, including Guy's and St Thomas' Hospital.

An advocate of Minimally-Invasive Aesthetic Dentistry, she holds qualifications including LDS RCS Eng, MFDS RCS Edin, a Certificate in Implantology (VSS Academy–UCLan University), and is working towards a PG Diploma in Implant Dentistry.
