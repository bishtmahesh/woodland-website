What It Is

The Closest Thing to a Natural Tooth

A dental implant is a small titanium post that replaces the root of a missing tooth. Once it has fused with the jawbone, a custom-made crown is fitted on top — giving you back a tooth that looks, feels, and functions like your own.

Implants are only ever placed once your gums and bone are healthy. This single rule, followed carefully by Dr. Sowjanya at every stage, is what prevents the vast majority of long-term implant failures.

Why Choose Implants

Why Patients Choose Implants

A permanent solution designed to protect the health of your whole mouth, not just replace a gap.

Natural Look & Feel

Custom ceramic crowns are shade-matched and shaped to blend seamlessly with your surrounding teeth.

Confident Eating

Fused firmly to the jawbone, implants restore full biting force so you can eat whatever you enjoy.

Clear Speech

Unlike removable dentures, a fixed implant crown stays perfectly in place, so speech stays natural.

Protects the Jawbone

The implant stimulates the bone just as a natural root would, preventing the bone loss dentures can cause.

Kind to Other Teeth

No need to grind down healthy neighbouring teeth, unlike a traditional bridge.

Built to Last

With good hygiene and regular check-ups, implants are designed to last for decades.
