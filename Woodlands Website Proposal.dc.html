08 — Who We Design For

Four Visitors, Four Journeys

The Nervous Patient

Has avoided the dentist for years. Needs reassurance before any mention of treatment.

UX answer: "Nervous? Start here" path from the hero, judgement-free copy.

The Smile Seeker

Researching bonding, whitening or braces. Compares practices on results and price.

UX answer: Smile Gallery and Fees & Plans one click from the nav.

The Emergency

In pain, on a phone, right now. Reads nothing — needs a number.

UX answer: tap-to-call emergency route visible on every mobile screen.

The Referrer

A fellow dentist referring for implants or CBCT imaging. Values speed and professionalism.

UX answer: dedicated Referral item in the nav with a short structured form.

09 — UX Strategy

Why Patients Choose Woodlands

Every section earns trust before the site ever asks for a booking — proof first, promises second.

Hospital-grade technology

Axano · CBCT · microscope — with manufacturer links patients can verify.

How: Dedicated Technology Showcase page, linked from the homepage and every treatment page.

Genuinely gentle

A real nervous-patient pathway — talk first, sedation, stop-anytime signal.

How: Nervous Patients page in the main nav, plus a hero link on every entry point.

Everything under one roof

Check-ups to implants, braces, aesthetics and same-day emergency care.

How: One unified nav — Treatments, Facial Aesthetics and Emergency sit side by side.

A journey, not a transaction

Patients see every step of their visit before they ever arrive.

How: Patient Journey section with a visual step-by-step, before the booking ask.

Proof, not promises

Real before & after cases, in the patient's own words.

How: Interactive Smile Gallery with filters, embedded on the homepage and its own page.

This is the story the "Why Choose Us" section tells — in order, on every visit.
