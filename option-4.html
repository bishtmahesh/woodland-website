A Woodlands Heritage

Specialist Orthodontics, Since Day One

Long before "comprehensive dentistry" became part of our name, Woodlands was already known across North Harrow for one thing: excellent orthodontics. That's the reputation we were built on, and it's still what we care about most.

As we've grown into a full dental centre, that heritage hasn't gone anywhere. Every brace, aligner and bite correction still follows the same specialist standard our patients have trusted for years. Dr Millen qualified in dentistry in Bristol in 1988, then trained in orthodontics at Guy's Hospital, gaining her Masters in Orthodontics and Membership from the Royal College of Surgeons. She's been Practice Principal since 2004, and was one of the first orthodontists in the UK trained in lingual orthodontics.

Meet Your Principal Dentist

Dr. Sowjanya Avireddi

Principal Dentist · LDS RCS Eng, MFDS RCS Edin

Dr Sowjanya qualified with her BDS in 2008, then spent over seven years building hospital experience in Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery and Oral Medicine at leading NHS Foundation Trusts, including Guy's and St Thomas' Hospital.

She's a firm believer in minimally invasive, aesthetic dentistry, and holds qualifications including LDS RCS Eng, MFDS RCS Edin and a Certificate in Implantology (VSS Academy–UCLan University), with a PG Diploma in Implant Dentistry currently underway.
