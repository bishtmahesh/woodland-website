The Woodlands Difference

Why Choose Woodlands Centre for Dentistry?

Comprehensive Care. Personalised to You.

Building on our long-standing heritage in orthodontic care, Woodlands Centre for Dentistry now offers a comprehensive range of trusted dental services, bringing modern dentistry together under one roof.

From preventive and general dentistry to Orthodontics & Invisalign, Dental Implants, Restorative Dentistry, Cosmetic Dentistry, Emergency Dentistry and Facial Aesthetics, every aspect of your care is thoughtfully tailored to your individual needs.

Our philosophy is simple: listen carefully, treat thoughtfully and provide dentistry you can trust.

Our Heritage

Built on a Long-Standing Orthodontic Heritage

Orthodontics has been part of Woodlands Centre for Dentistry for more than four decades. We provide orthodontic treatment for children, teenagers and adults — the appropriate treatment depends on the individual's dental health, orthodontic needs, age and treatment objectives.

Children's & Adult Orthodontics

Orthodontic-led care for every age, from early assessment through to adult treatment.

Fixed & Removable Braces

Fixed braces and removable orthodontic appliances tailored to the individual's treatment plan.

Clear Aligners & Invisalign

Discreet clear aligner treatment, including Invisalign, for patients who prefer a less visible option.

Orthodontic-Led Care

An assessment allows our team to discuss the options available and explain the benefits and considerations of each.

Our Patients

A Practice Shaped by Relationships, Growth & Clear Communication

Long-Term Relationships

With approximately 15,000 patients in our database, we've cared for people across every stage of life — creating an environment where patients can ask questions and understand their treatment choices.

Expanding Private Dentistry

We continue to develop our range of private dental services in Harrow, from routine oral healthcare through to restorative, cosmetic and orthodontic treatment — building on more than 40 years of experience.

Care Based on Communication

Before treatment, we discuss the relevant options, explain what's involved and answer your questions — including risks, benefits, alternatives and costs where appropriate — so you can take part in decisions about your own care.

Looking Ahead

The Future of Woodlands Centre for Dentistry

More than 40 years of orthodontic care and approximately 15,000 patients form an important part of our story. At the same time, we continue to develop our wider private dentistry offering in response to the needs of today's patients. We want Woodlands Centre for Dentistry in Harrow to remain a practice where patients can access clear information, discuss their options and receive care appropriate to their individual circumstances.

Woodlands Centre for Dentistry brings together more than four decades of orthodontic history with the continued development of comprehensive private dentistry. Whether you have been part of the Woodlands patient community for many years or are considering the practice for the first time, our focus is on providing clear information, discussing appropriate treatment options and supporting you throughout your dental care.

Meet Your Principal Dentist

Dr. Sowjanya Avireddi

Principal Dentist · LDS RCS Eng, MFDS RCS Edin

Dr Sowjanya qualified with her BDS in 2008, then spent over seven years building hospital experience in Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery and Oral Medicine at leading NHS Foundation Trusts, including Guy's and St Thomas' Hospital.

She's a firm believer in minimally invasive, aesthetic dentistry, and holds qualifications including LDS RCS Eng, MFDS RCS Edin and a Certificate in Implantology (VSS Academy–UCLan University), with a PG Diploma in Implant Dentistry currently underway.
