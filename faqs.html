Full-Arch Restorations (All-on-4)

A full-arch restoration replaces all, or nearly all, of the teeth in your upper or lower jaw with a fixed bridge supported by a small number of dental implants. It is designed to look, feel and function like a natural set of teeth.

This depends on your individual bone quality and treatment plan, but a full arch is often supported by as few as four to six implants. Your dentist will confirm the appropriate number following a 3D CBCT assessment.

Many denture wearers are suitable candidates, though bone volume can change over time following tooth loss. An assessment, including a 3D scan, will confirm whether you have sufficient bone or whether additional preparatory treatment is needed.

In many cases a temporary fixed bridge can be fitted on the day implants are placed, so you are not left without teeth while healing takes place. Your dentist will discuss what is appropriate for your circumstances.

Treatment typically involves an initial healing period after implant placement, followed by fitting of your final restoration. Timescales vary between patients, and your dentist will explain the stages and expected timeframe during your consultation.

The cost of full-arch restorations depends on your individual treatment plan, including the number of implants required and any preparatory treatment. Your dentist will provide a detailed, personalised quote following your assessment. Please see our Fees page for further information.
